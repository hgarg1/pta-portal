Low-pressure ways to support

  • Share PTA events in your classroom chats or carpool groups.
  • Ask your workplace about matching gifts for school fundraisers.
  • Drop a thank-you note in the office for a favorite staff member.
  • Attend one PTA meeting this semester to stay in the loop.

Recurring opportunities

  • Lunchroom Buddy rotation - 45-minute shifts assisting grades K-2.
  • Library shelving crew - help Mrs. Owens re-shelve books on Fridays.
  • Garden keepers - adopt a planter box for monthly watering and weeding.

Student Notes

What is happening at our school this week?

This page is just for you. See the fun stuff that is coming up and how you can join in.

This week's fun things

  • Book Fair Night - Come to the library with your grown-ups to look at books.
  • Walk and Roll to School - Meet other students and walk in together from the front parking lot.
  • Teacher Snack Cart - The PTA is surprising teachers with snacks.
  • Chess Club Practice - Meet in the Innovation Lab on Thursday.
  • Spirit Wear Friday - Wear your Riverbend colors or favorite school shirt.

Coming up soon

  • Family Movie Night - Wear comfy clothes, bring a blanket, and watch a movie in the gym.
  • STEM Night - Try science and tech stations around the school.
  • Winter Concert and Art Walk - Sing, play instruments, or show your artwork.
  • Holiday Giving Tree - Choose an ornament with a wish list item to help a Riverbend family.
  • Garden Clean-Up Day - Help plant bulbs and pull weeds with your family.

Before you go

  • Ask a grown-up who is picking you up after the event and where to meet them.
  • Pack a labeled water bottle and jacket in case the gym or playground feels chilly.
  • Charge your Chromebook if you need it for STEM Night stations or homework afterward.

Need help? Use the student help form and a counselor or PTA volunteer will follow up.
